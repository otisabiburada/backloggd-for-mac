# Privacy

- The app does not collect, store or send any data to its author or any third party.
- It loads **backloggd.com** (and the images, fonts and scripts that site uses) in a web view,
  exactly as a browser would. Backloggd's own privacy policy applies to that traffic.
- Your Backloggd login cookies and the app's settings are stored locally on your Mac
  (in the app's WebKit data store and `UserDefaults`). Delete the app's data to remove them.
- Notifications are generated locally: the app checks the site's notification bell while it runs
  and shows a macOS notification. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- Custom CSS, if you use it, is a plain file in `~/Library/Application Support/Backloggd/`.
- The app does not check for updates by itself. New versions are published on the GitHub Releases page.
